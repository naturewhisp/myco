## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the contemplative rigor of classical botanical monographs, historic natural history folios, and handwritten field herbariums. Built specifically for mycological forecasting, foraging, and taxonomic research, the visual language treats the digital screen not as an emissive pane of glass, but as archival parchment pressed with iron-gall sepia ink and mineral pigments.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Scholarly & Deliberate:** The atmosphere evokes a 19th-century naturalist expedition ledger. Every typographic scale, tabular rule, and status indicator reflects scientific discipline rather than generic dashboard utility.
- **Organic & Grounded:** By replacing artificial gradients, drop shadows, and synthetic neons with raw earth pigments—salvia, ocra, terracotta, ruggine, and bosco—the product feels intrinsically tethered to woodland ecology.
- **Utilitarian Field Tool:** Designed for high legibility under direct forest sunlight, during wet autumn rains, and across rocky terrains. Interactions favor immediate cognitive legibility over ornamental clutter.

### Design Movement: Naturalist Minimalism & Archival Tactility
The interface repudiates modern software tropes such as glassmorphism, floating elevation cards, and heavy container boxes. Depth is entirely flat and textual, structured strictly through typographic hierarchy and delicate horizontal hairlines. The paper itself is the container. Elements sit openly on the canvas like annotated botanical specimens pressed onto a specimen sheet.

## Colors

Color within the design system serves pure taxonomy and environmental context. Rather than utilizing arbitrary decorative accents, colors are mapped to strict botanical thresholds, meteorological variables, and organic surfaces.

### Surface and Typography
- **Parchment (`#F4EDE0`) & Night Base (`#221E18`):** The grounding tactile background that replaces pristine digital whites and blacks.
- **Parchment Variant (`#E8DECB`) & Night Surface (`#2E2820`):** Used sparingly for secondary grouping zones without creating boxed card containers.
- **Ink Primary (`#3B2F26`) & Night Ink (`#EDE3D2`):** A warm, deep sepia ink formulation achieving an ultra-high 11:1 WCAG AAA contrast ratio on parchment, eliminating eye strain during prolonged outdoor use.
- **Ink Secondary (`#6E5B49`) & Night Ink Soft (`#B6A891`):** Subdued pigment for taxonomic classifications, coordinates, and contextual metadata.

### The Five-Tier Taxonomic Scale
Probability tiers, growth favorability, and danger warnings follow a chromatic scale across both light and dark spectrums:
- **Tier 0 (< 20% | Empty / Inactive):** `#EFE7D6` (Light) / `#3A3128` (Dark)
- **Tier 1 (20–39% | Emergent / Salvia):** `#BCC7A6` (Light) / `#6B7A55` (Dark)
- **Tier 2 (40–59% | Moderate / Ocra):** `#C89B3C` (Light) / `#DCB65C` (Dark)
- **Tier 3 (60–74% | High / Terracotta):** `#B96F42` (Light) / `#C67C4B` (Dark)
- **Tier 4 (75–100% | Peak / Critical / Ruggine):** `#9B4A38` (Light) / `#B85A45` (Dark)

### Functional and Meteorological Accents
- **Forest (`#3F5136`) & Lichen (`#6F8355`):** Reserved for favorable symbiotic environmental states, primary action triggers, and active location pins.
- **Indigo (`#4E6273`) & Night Indigo (`#8AA3B5`):** Exclusively designated for atmospheric metrics: precipitation sums, relative soil humidity, barometric trends, and lunar phases.

## Typography

The dual typographic voice creates tension between literary scholarship and field practicality.

### Typographic Roles
- **Editorial Voice (Newsreader):** Used for primary locations, scientific binomial nomenclature (*Boletus edulis*), probability score indicators, and historical field annotations (*Note di campo*). Its humanist optical proportion honors classical typography without compromising digital rendering.
- **Instrumental UI Voice (Source Sans 3):** Used for factor titles, numerical tables, sensor logs, and interactive toggles. Neutral, utilitarian, and legible when quickly scanned outdoors.
- **Geographic Data (Courier Prime):** Applied to technical readouts, coordinates (WGS84), and elevation datums.

### Scaling & Accessibility Directives
- **Container Sizing:** Fixed element heights are strictly prohibited for text nodes. UI containers must use minimum height boundaries (`min-height`) to expand naturally up to 200% system accessibility scale without clipping.
- **Uppercase Overlines:** `label-md` utilizes an expanded tracking of `0.08em` for field classification tags, creating a clean distinction between system metadata and human notes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout in this design system rejects nested cards and boxed containers. Surfaces are conceived as single, uninterrupted sheets of manuscript paper structured by strict linear alignment, tabular right-aligned data, and horizontal dividing rules.

### Layout Model
- **Manuscript Grid:** Mobile screens run on an edge-padded single-column flow with `1.125rem` (`18px`) lateral margins. Desktop and tablet environments conform to a centered folio layout capped at `60rem` (`960px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins.
- **Tabular Right-Alignment:** Metric names and factor icons align flush to the left rule boundary; critical values, probability tiers, and status descriptors align rigorously to the right hairline margin.
- **Prose Constraints:** To safeguard reading cadence, editorial field notes are capped at `74ch`, and section subtitles at `62ch`.

### Field Interaction Rules
- **Non-Negotiable Touch Target:** Every interactive element adheres to a minimum dimension of `3rem` (`48px`) across both height and width (`touch-min`). This guarantees precise tactile triggering when operated with one hand, while wearing wet weather gear, or under field conditions.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system operates on an uncompromising **Zero-Shadow, Completely Flat** elevation philosophy.

### Flat Stratification
- **No Cast Shadows:** `box-shadow` and drop shadows are entirely prohibited across buttons, dialogs, drawers, and controls. Shadows are considered digital artifice alien to flat botanical folios.
- **Tonal Stratification:** Visual stacking is achieved through flat, solid fills. A modal or bottom sheet uses Parchment Variant (`#E8DECB` / `#2E2820`) overlaid against Parchment (`#F4EDE0` / `#221E18`), separated solely by a solid `1px` Hairline Divider (`#B3A188` / `#5C5245`).
- **Absence of Glassmorphism:** Translucent blur effects (`backdrop-filter`) are banned. Surface transitions remain immediate and fully opaque.

### Backdrop Scrims & Map Overlays
- **Backdrop Scrim:** Modal overlays use a warm, flat wash of ink at 50% opacity in light mode (`rgba(59, 47, 38, 0.50)`), and deep black at 60% opacity in dark mode (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.60)`).
- **Cartographic Probability Layers:** Geospatial heatmaps sit directly over contour charts using a flat opacity of `128/255` in light mode and `150/255` in dark mode, preserving the legibility of geographic place names beneath taxonomic overlays.

## Shapes

The shape geometry balances classic bookbinding corners with ergonomic digital touch boundaries.

### Contour Philosophy
- **Soft Sharpness (`roundedness: 1`):** All standard components use subtle corner radii (`0.25rem` to `0.375rem`). This avoids the toy-like quality of pill-shaped containers while softening hard brutalist corners into something resembling cut archival rag paper.
- **Segmented Meters:** Micro meters and segmented probability indicators use a refined `3px` corner radius.
- **Bottom Sheets:** Mobile sheets and contextual menus employ `1rem` (`16px`) radii exclusively on the top two corners to present an unobtrusive upward pull sheet.

## Components

Components discard all decorative housing in favor of stark, rule-delimited data structures.

### Structural Dividers & Ornaments
- **Hairlines (`MycoDivider`):** A single, solid `1px` rule using `rule-subtle` (`#D8CDB8` light, `#3E372E` dark). It serves as the primary separator between rows, sections, and headers.
- **Botanical Break:** Major thematic section breaks incorporate an unembellished linear botanical glyph (`✳` or `Icons.Outlined.Spa`) measuring `16px`, centered within a broken horizontal rule.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Styled in Forest green (`#3F5136`) with Parchment typography (`#F4EDE0`), using a `0.375rem` (`6px`) radius, zero shadow, and a mandatory minimum height of `48px`.
- **Secondary / Outlined Button:** Transparent fill bounded by a `1px` solid Hairline (`#B3A188`), displaying Ink Primary text. Active state shifts surface to Parchment Variant.
- **Tactile State Feedback:** Interactive states rely solely on flat color shifts—never elevation or lift.

### Factor Rows & Taxonomic Lists
- **Card-Less Factor Row:** Every environmental factor (humidity, rainfall, soil acidity, mycelial network status) is laid out within a frameless row separated by subtle hairlines.
- **Layout:** Outlined monochrome icon (`20px`) on the left, followed by the factor label in `body-md`, and terminating in a right-aligned metric value alongside a five-tier colored probability pip or label.
- **Warning Strips:** Cautionary notes avoid background banners; they feature a `2px` left-border accent in Terracotta (`#B96F42`) paired with editorial italic serif text.

### Segmented Probability Bars
- **Taxonomic Probability Meter:** Composed of five distinct horizontal segments separated by a `4px` gap. Segments up to the current calculated score fill with the corresponding taxonomic tier pigment (`scale-0` through `scale-4`), while inactive segments adopt the neutral empty tint (`#EFE7D6` / `#3A3128`).

### Input Fields
- **Parchment Input:** Replaces enclosed text boxes with a clean underline or a `1px` hairline boundary in `rule-hairline`. Input text renders in deep ink, with placeholder text in soft sepia. Focused state switches the hairline boundary to Forest (`#3F5136`) without glow rings.
